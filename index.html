What we do

Each engagement is a defined package with a clear deliverable and a fixed price agreed before work begins. Packages can stand alone or run in sequence from first CDMO conversation to submission.

Approach

CMC documents are only as strong as the science behind them. Our work draws on hands-on experience at the bench, in the cleanroom and in the submission itself.

Defined scope, fixed price

You know what you will receive and what it will cost before work starts. No open-ended hours.

Phase-appropriate

Early-stage programs need the right level of control, not late-stage requirements applied too soon. We size the work to the phase.

Confidential by default

We work under NDA and, where you prefer, inside your own systems. Your data and documents stay under your control.

Document quality tools, in development

We are building tools that check regulatory documents for consistency in terminology, formatting and cross-document details. They run entirely in your environment. Your documents never leave it.

Founder

Hua Jin founded HSR Bio after more than a decade across cell biology research, life science products, and biologics development from antibody discovery through first-in-human.

She has led four INDs from strategy through submission to FDA, authoring content across Modules 2, 3 and 4. At SparX Biopharmaceutical (2021–2025) she served as regulatory lead and directed CMC and GMP operations, cell line development including a GS-knockout line, cleanroom remediation and GMP readiness for a first manufacturing campaign, and antibody discovery using phage, yeast and mammalian display.
